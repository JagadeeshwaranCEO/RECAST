## Product synthesis

The research led to five connected systems instead of sixteen disconnected imitation tools:

1. **Brand OS** — visual tokens, voice, locked rules and a shared control plane.
2. **Agent Council** — six accountable agents working from one governed brief and approved proof.
3. **Production Studio** — owned-asset ingestion, editable poster layouts and production PNG export.
4. **Launch Control** — a working campaign destination, channel packages, timing and a human activation boundary.
5. **Signal Radar** — listening, sentiment, risk and an approved insight-to-brief learning loop.

## Data and rights policy

RECAST does not train on, republish, or scrape protected campaign creative into the repository. Historical campaign sources are used as a cited retrieval and strategy layer. User assets must be owned or licensed. A production ingestion service must preserve creator, licence, territory, expiry and usage-right metadata, and must support takedown and retention controls.

External tools are shown only as researched workflow references or explicit connector opportunities. OAuth/API access, platform review and server-side credentials are required before any third-party publishing or monitoring can be described as active.
